# No shared protocol for BriefingScheduler and VisionCheckupScheduler

`BriefingScheduler` and `VisionCheckupScheduler` are not abstracted behind a shared `PeriodicAITask` protocol despite both being coordinator-owned AI schedulers with `async_stop()`. The startup signatures are asymmetric — `BriefingScheduler.start()` registers facility-wide interval and entity-change listeners, while `VisionCheckupScheduler.schedule_all_growspaces()` sets per-growspace timers at light-cycle offsets. These are genuinely different operations and forcing them behind a shared interface would either lose the expressive startup names or make `start()` mean something different across the two adapters. With only two stable schedulers the seam is hypothetical, not real. If a third AI scheduler is ever added, revisit this decision at that point.
