# Water balance and feed strength are separate control levers

Irrigation timing and volume control substrate water balance, while Feed Intent controls solution EC and pH; neither is treated as a substitute for the other. The irrigation suite owns both intents but dosing is an optional capability, allowing hand-mixed hobby feed and verified facility dosing to use the same model. Runoff and pore-EC evidence may recommend bounded changes to either lever, but low-confidence or unattributed runoff never drives immediate shot-to-shot correction.
