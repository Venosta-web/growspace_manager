# The Daily Caps Enforce a Durable Dispensed Volume, Not the Water Figure

**Status:** Accepted — not yet implemented. Item 4's storage is replaced by [ADR-0055](0055-delivery-attempts-are-the-durable-record-of-every-pump-request.md); the rule is unchanged.

Growspace Manager kept two unreconciled answers to "how much water today" (#546). The daily cycle limit and daily volume cap enforced an in-memory counter, `_cycles_today` / `_volume_dispensed_today`, which a restart set back to zero mid-day. The card displayed [[Aggregate Water Use]] (ADR-0017), which is persisted but, in [[Tank-Derived Water Mode]], measures reservoir drop rather than pump runtime. The card showed both, as "Dispensed today" and "Liters today", with nothing to say why they differed.

They are two concepts, and they stay two. The caps enforce **[[Dispensed Volume]]**: the pump volume and pump starts the [[Irrigation Controller]] has itself charged today. [[Aggregate Water Use]] stays the one answer to how much water the plants got and is never enforced on. A limit has to be computable before the shot it decides and must not move with a sensor. A reservoir drop cannot be known until after the shot, lags it, and also counts leaks and hand-draws from the tank. So in tank mode the tank figure wins for display and the pump figure wins for enforcement, and neither is ever averaged or maxed with the other.

1. **Day.** Both figures use local midnight in Home Assistant's timezone, as ADR-0017 and ADR-0049 already do. A lights-on day was rejected: with an 18/6 schedule and lights on at 18:00, a midnight reset lets up to twice the cap flow within one photoperiod. That is still bounded, and the cap is a runaway backstop rather than a dosing budget, which steering owns. A lights-on day also does not exist for a time-based growspace without a light schedule, and two boundaries would make the two figures disagree for reasons nobody can see.
2. **What is charged.** Every irrigation cycle whose pump confirmed ON charges one cycle and its volume, whether automatic or a manual pump run, and whether it finished or was aborted. An aborted start was still a start. Hand-watering from a separate source, drain cycles, and an [[Unconfirmed Pump Cycle]] (already a fault path with its own latch) charge nothing.
3. **How much.** A shot is charged its **planned** volume the moment the pump confirms ON, and topped up at the end if the pump ran longer than planned. The [[Pump-Cycle Water Estimate]] charges the same shot's **measured** ON time. The two therefore differ on purpose: an aborted 5 s shot out of a 60 s plan costs the cap 60 s and shows 5 s of water. The cap errs toward having counted water; the display errs toward the truth.
4. **Durability.** The figure is stored per growspace as `{date, cycles, liters}` in `IrrigationSafetyStore`, and the write reaches disk before the cycle proceeds. That store is kept apart from debounced configuration saves, and no user reset can clear it. When it is unreadable it already holds every cycle, which is also the correct answer for a cap whose history is unknown. Because charging happens at confirm-ON, a restart in the middle of a shot keeps that shot counted. On load the record is restored only when its `date` is today, which is the ADR-0049 pattern: yesterday's record simply never matches, so the midnight reset has no stored state to clear. Charges are dated by the day the pump confirmed ON; a top-up that lands after midnight belongs to a day that is no longer enforced. A missed shot is never replayed; the [[Startup Inhibit]] already guarantees that, and restoring a counter replays nothing.
5. **Disagreement.** When the tank drop and the pump volume diverge materially, the cause is usually a wrong `pump_flow_rate_ml_per_sec`, a leak, or a draw from the tank. A flow rate configured too low leaves the cap looser than the grower believes, so a material disagreement **must be surfaced as a calibration signal and never enforced**. Its threshold, wording and placement belong to the flow-meter and calibration-confidence work on the irrigation map (#544), which waits on the delivery lifecycle (#549).
6. **Card.** Dispensed Volume is presented against the cap it counts toward, e.g. "Toward daily cap 3.2 / 10 L · 4 / 12 cycles", or as a plain "Dispensed today" when no cap is set. "Liters today" stays the Aggregate Water Use. This fixes what each tile means; the card work itself belongs to the map's card-surface item.

## Considered Options

- **Enforce on Aggregate Water Use.** One number, but in tank mode the cap would ride a lagging sensor that counts water the pump never moved and can move either way on noise. A limit that can loosen because a tank reading dipped is not a limit.
- **Enforce on the larger of the pump figure and the tank figure.** Safer against a flow rate configured too low, but a leak or a hand-draw from the tank would block irrigation. The flow-rate risk is a calibration problem and is handled as one (item 5).
- **Persist the cap figure in `WaterUsageData`.** It is already stored and dated, but `reset_water_tracking` clears it mid-day, so one service call would lift the cap. Tank mode also never writes the pump estimate there.
- **Keep charging at the end of the cycle.** This is what the code does today, and a restart mid-shot loses the shot from the cap.

## Consequences

- `IrrigationSafetyStore` gains a per-growspace daily record, and the [[Pump Cycle Gate]] reads it instead of the coordinator's counters. The gate itself stays pure.
- The Pump-Cycle Water Estimate stops using the planned-or-longer figure, so pump-estimate totals for growspaces with aborted shots will read lower than before.
- #549 made the Delivery Attempt store the source Dispensed Volume is derived from, replacing the `{date, cycles, liters}` record of item 4 (ADR-0055). Everything else here stands.
